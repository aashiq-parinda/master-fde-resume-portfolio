## 2. Domestic Indian Tax Structure

Unlike Projects 1 and 3 (which are structured as cross-border exports), CareFirst is a domestic Indian client, meaning standard local commercial laws apply:
*   **Goods and Services Tax (GST)**: Subject to **18% GST** for professional IT services. The invoice must clearly display the IGST/CGST split. GST is added on top of the contract value and paid by the client.
*   **Tax Deducted at Source (TDS)**: CareFirst deducts **10% TDS** under **Section 194J** (Fees for Professional or Technical Services) of the Indian Income Tax Act. This TDS is credited to the provider's Form 26AS to offset year-end corporate tax liabilities.
