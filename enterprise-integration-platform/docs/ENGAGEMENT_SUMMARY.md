*   **Headquarters Country**: Munich, Germany

## 3. Multi-Country Payment & Tax Structure

*   **Contractual Framework**: Master Services Agreement (MSA) signed with Optima Global Components GmbH (Germany). Work orders executed locally to handle network permissions in Munich, London, and Mumbai sites.
*   **Tax Considerations**:
    *   **GST (Export of Services)**: Since the client is in Germany, professional services are treated as an **Export of Services** under Indian GST laws. This is a **Zero-Rated Supply** (0% GST applies) provided an LUT is filed, and payments are received in convertible foreign currency.
    *   **German Withholding Tax (WHT)**: Under Article 12 of the **India-Germany DTAA**, a 10% withholding tax applies on Fees for Technical Services (FTS). Optima Global Components deducts 10% WHT at source, which the Indian provider offsets locally as a Foreign Tax Credit.
    *   **Currency**: The project is denominated in INR, with invoices billed and settled in Euros (EUR) at the Spot FX rate on milestone completion.
